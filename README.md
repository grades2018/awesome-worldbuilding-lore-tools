## AI Writing Suites with World Continuity

Long-form platforms that can draft manuscripts while holding world rules, cast, and setting notes.

- ⭐ [ImagineYourBook](https://www.imagineyourbook.com/) - Book workspace that plans and drafts full manuscripts, keeps world rules and cast in a series story bible, and finishes stalled drafts in the author's voice.
- ⭐ [Sudowrite](https://www.sudowrite.com/) - AI fiction suite with Story Bible, brainstorming, and prose drafting for genre novelists.
- ⭐ [NovelCrafter](https://www.novelcrafter.com/) - Novel planning and prose workspace with a Codex for characters, places, factions, and lore.
- [Campfire](https://www.campfirewriting.com/) - Modular writing suite with modules for magic systems, species, cultures, and manuscripts.
- [Squibler](https://www.squibler.io/) - Book writing platform with AI drafting, outlines, and manuscript organization.
- [NovelAI](https://novelai.net/) - Storytelling-focused AI with lorebooks for prose generation and fiction experimentation.
- [LivingWriter](https://livingwriter.com/) - Novel writing app with outlines, boards, and story elements for long projects.

## Worldbuilding Wikis and Encyclopedias

Encyclopedia-style tools for settings, histories, religions, factions, and public lore pages.

- ⭐ [World Anvil](https://www.worldanvil.com/) - Worldbuilding wiki with templates for countries, species, magic, timelines, and family trees.
- [LegendKeeper](https://www.legendkeeper.com/) - Visual wiki and map notebook for building connected settings fast.
- [Kanka](https://kanka.io/) - Worldbuilding and campaign wiki for characters, locations, organizations, and calendars.
- [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) - Linked-note vault popular for lore wikis, character sheets, and continuity graphs.
- [Notion](https://www.notion.so/) - Flexible workspace often used as a custom world wiki and series bible.
- [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/) - Long-form writing app with corkboard, research folders, and binder for lore notes.

## Series Bibles and Lore Continuity

Keeping kingdoms, bloodlines, magic rules, and cast arcs straight across multiple books.

- ⭐ [ImagineYourBook](https://www.imagineyourbook.com/) - Series story bible that carries characters, arcs, open threads, and invented terms into the next volume.
- [NovelCrafter](https://www.novelcrafter.com/) - Series Codex shared across books so lore and character progressions stay in sync.
- [Plottr](https://plottr.com/) - Visual timeline and template-based plotter for series arcs and multi-POV outlines.
- [Dabble](https://www.dabblewriter.com/) - Cloud novel writing app with plot grids, story notes, and goals.
- [RelationCanvas](https://relationcanvas.com/) - Free browser tool for character relationship charts with a separate label for each direction; PNG export.

## Maps and Cartography

Map makers for endpapers, reader reference, GM navigation, and your own exploration.

- [Inkarnate](https://inkarnate.com/) - Browser-based fantasy map maker for world, region, and city maps.
- [Wonderdraft](https://wonderdraft.net/) - Desktop map creation tool for detailed fantasy world maps.
- [Azgaar's Fantasy Map Generator](https://azgaar.github.io/Fantasy-Map-Generator/) - Free open source generator for procedural worlds with states, cultures, and rivers.
- [Dungeon Scrawl](https://dungeonscrawl.com/) - Free tool for quick floor plans of castles, dungeons, and buildings.
- [Dungeon Fog](https://www.dungeonfog.com/) - Map creator for battle maps, regions, and world overviews.

## Names, Languages and Generators

Help with names, words, and constructed languages that make a world feel lived-in.

- [Fantasy Name Generators](https://www.fantasynamegenerators.com/) - Huge free collection of name generators for races, places, and objects.
- [Mingzi](https://mingzi.io/chinese-name-generator) - Authentic Chinese character name generator and surname directory with meanings, tones, and five-element balance for character naming.
- [Name Awesome](https://nameawesome.com/) - Real U.S. first names by year and decade since 1880, with meanings and origins, for era-appropriate character names.
- [Vulgarlang](https://www.vulgarlang.com/) - Conlang generator for consistent fictional languages, words, and grammar.
- [Donjon](https://donjon.bin.sh/) - Classic generators for names, calendars, weather, and dungeon content.
- [Chaotic Shiny](https://chaoticshiny.com/) - Generators for cultures, religions, plots, and setting details.

## Timelines and Chronology

Structure and chronology for histories, wars, dynasties, and multi-POV epics.

- [Aeon Timeline](https://www.aeontimeline.com/) - Timeline software for tracking events, ages, and chronology across a world's history.
- [Timeline JS](https://timeline.knightlab.com/) - Open-source tool for publishing interactive historical timelines.
- [Plottr](https://plottr.com/) - Series timelines and beat templates for multi-book chronology.

## Editing and Manuscript Rewrite

Revision and critique once the world and draft exist on the page.

- ⭐ [ImagineYourBook](https://www.imagineyourbook.com/) - Manuscript Rewrite for polishing or restructuring a full draft while keeping the author's voice and world terms.
- [ProWritingAid](https://prowritingaid.com/) - Style and manuscript reports for catching repetition, pacing, and consistency issues.
- [AutoCrit](https://www.autocrit.com/) - Fiction-focused manuscript critique with genre-aware analysis.
- [Marlowe (Authors.ai)](https://authors.ai/marlowe/) - Analytical AI manuscript feedback on plot, pacing, and character.

## Formatting and Export

Getting the finished book into ebook and print shape.

- ⭐ [ImagineYourBook](https://www.imagineyourbook.com/) - Reader and editor screen for formatting and line edits, with export to EPUB, Word, and Markdown.
- [Atticus](https://www.atticus.io/) - Cross-platform book formatter for ebook and print interiors.
- [Vellum](https://vellum.pub/) - Mac book formatting software with polished genre styles.
- [Reedsy Studio](https://reedsy.com/studio) - Free writing and formatting studio.

## Covers and Titles

Covers and titles that signal the setting and subgenre at a glance.

- ⭐ [ImagineYourBook](https://www.imagineyourbook.com/) - Cover and title engine for unique covers and title ideas matched to the manuscript.
- [BookBrush](https://bookbrush.com/) - Author graphics studio for 3D covers, mockups, and promo images.
- [Canva](https://www.canva.com/) - Design tool with AI helpers for promo graphics and social assets.
- [Midjourney](https://www.midjourney.com/) - Image generation often used for concept art and cover mood boards.

## General AI Assistants

General-purpose models worldbuilders use for lore Q&A, culture sketches, and consistency checks.

- [Claude](https://www.anthropic.com/claude) - Long-context AI assistant for lore checks, chapter feedback, and brainstorming.
- [ChatGPT](https://openai.com/chatgpt/overview) - General AI chatbot for brainstorming cultures, magic systems, and plot options.
